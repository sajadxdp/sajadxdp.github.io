Hi. I'm Sajad.

I'm a software engineer. I design, build, and maintain the backends that AI products run on.

How I work

Most days it's Python: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, and the plumbing that moves work between them. I own the architecture I write and the operations after it. When something breaks, the first thing I look for is the missing test.

I like boring internals and clean interfaces. A repo should tell you what it does and then get out of the way; if it needs a sales pitch, it isn't finished.

On the side

I study security one topic at a time, on both the attack and defense sides, and what I learn tends to leave as a tool rather than a note. vpnbox, a free Linux VPN built into a single stdlib-only Python file, started exactly that way.

Right now

The vpnbox beta is out (v1.0.0-beta4), and Lexora is next in line for hardening. Also on GitHub: HookFlow (webhooks that never get lost) and neuDB (a tiny crash-atomic database).

Longer notes live on the Writings page, and Contact has every way to reach me.
